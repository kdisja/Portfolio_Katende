Featured Work

Research built around health data, place, and public impact.

Infectious Disease Spatial Epidemiology

Onchocerciasis MDA Coverage

Investigating the spatial distribution of and systemic barriers to mass drug administration coverage across 238 clusters in Southern Malawi. The work uses Global Moran's I and statistical models that account for cluster-level and spatial dependence, with dashboards designed to highlight district-level variation.

Supporting targeted interventions and more effective allocation of public health resources.

Environmental Health Linked Data

Air Pollution and Cancer in Canada

Linked national household surveys, cancer registry data, and multiple Canadian Census Health and Environment Cohorts to build analytical datasets exceeding 100 million rows. Applied Cox proportional hazards, multilevel, and geospatial methods to investigate associations between air pollution and eight cancer outcomes.

Large-scale health data management, spatial analysis, and evidence for policymakers.

Child Health Survival Analysis

Smart Discharges

Supported predictive research on post-discharge outcomes among children under five with sepsis in East Africa. Used survival and geospatial analyses to investigate predictors of mortality and recovery and inform region-specific interventions.

Translating clinical and geographic evidence into more responsive follow-up care.

Occupational Health Peer-reviewed Research

Carcinogen Exposures Among New Workers

Co-authored a national study examining the overrepresentation of new workers in jobs with multiple carcinogen exposures in Canada, contributing analysis to a clearer understanding of occupational exposure inequities.
